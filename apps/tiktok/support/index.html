DreamStar Creator Studio (https://dreamstar.ai.kr/apps/tiktok/) provides web-based short-form video management and direct publishing to TikTok.

Account & Permission Management

To disconnect your TikTok account at any time, click the Disconnect button in the studio header or revoke access from the TikTok mobile app under Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions.

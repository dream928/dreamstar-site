Privacy Policy

Service URL: https://dreamstar.ai.kr/apps/tiktok/

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect via TikTok APIs

When you connect your TikTok account to DreamStar Creator Studio (https://dreamstar.ai.kr/apps/tiktok/) using TikTok Login Kit, we collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the publishing workflow:

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Display name, username, and avatar URL to display your connected TikTok identity in the publishing studio.
  • Creator Publishing Metadata (video.publish, video.upload): Privacy level options, comment/duet/stitch availability, and maximum allowed video duration returned by the TikTok Creator Info API.
  • OAuth 2.0 Credentials: Access and refresh tokens required to authenticate your video publishing requests.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to authenticate your session and transmit videos, captions, interaction settings, and privacy preferences that you explicitly submit to TikTok. DreamStar Creator Studio never sells, rents, or shares your personal data or OAuth tokens with third parties.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Uploaded video files are transmitted directly to TikTok's servers and are not permanently archived after publishing completes. When you click "Disconnect" in the dashboard at https://dreamstar.ai.kr/apps/tiktok/, all stored OAuth tokens and session data associated with your account are immediately removed.

4. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, please visit our Support page.
